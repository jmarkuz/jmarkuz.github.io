01 — About

Owning the lifecycle, from requirements to production.

I started in investment banking, building trade-processing and regulatory-reporting software for an international bank, then moved into telecom and iGaming platforms, where integration-heavy backends are the norm: carrier APIs, MDM/UEM providers, payment gateways and regulatory feeds.

My work centres on microservices, messaging and data stores (RabbitMQ, Kafka, PostgreSQL, Redis) and on the quieter parts that keep systems healthy: caching and query tuning, Kubernetes resource tuning, framework and runtime upgrades, and tests and documentation people actually use.

I have led a team of Java developers, run code reviews and technical design, and I am comfortable tracking down a production issue that crosses service boundaries.
